Getting Started

HR staff log in through the Staff Login portal. Upon login, HR Managers are directed to the HR Dashboard which provides access to all people-management tools: employee records, leave management, attendance monitoring, shift scheduling, appraisals, and payroll. All functions are role-gated — this guide covers the full HR Manager permission set.

1

Logging In as HR Staff

Navigate to the SweetTooth portal and use the Staff Login to access the HR module.

  1. Open your browser and go to the SweetTooth portal URL provided by your IT team.
  2. Click Login as Staff on the landing page.
  3. Select your branch from the Select Branch dropdown (e.g., SweetTooth Port Harcourt).
  4. Enter your HR email and password, then click Log in.
  5. You will be redirected to your HR Dashboard automatically.
ℹ️
Admin Login: If you have admin-level access you can also use the I am an admin link on the staff login page to access the full admin portal at /login.
Fig 1 — Staff login page. Select your branch before entering credentials.
📊

HR Dashboard

The HR Dashboard is your central hub. It displays summary cards for key HR metrics and provides the sidebar navigation for every HR function. The dashboard adapts to your role — HR Managers see all management tools while standard staff see only their personal leave and attendance pages.

1

Reading the HR Dashboard

After login you land on the HR Dashboard showing headcount, leave, and attendance summaries.

  • Summary cards show: total employees, staff on leave today, clock-ins today, and pending approvals.
  • The left sidebar is your navigation — sections are grouped by function (Employees, Leave, Attendance, Shifts, Appraisals, Payroll, Reports).
  • Quick action buttons at the top allow fast access to common tasks.
Fig 2 — HR Dashboard showing summary metrics and the full navigation sidebar.
👥

Employee Directory

The Employee Directory is the master list of all staff at your branch. It supports advanced filtering, bulk actions, and CSV export. From here you can view any employee's profile, jump to their edit form, or trigger role assignment workflows.

1

Browsing and Filtering Employees

Navigate to All Employees in the sidebar to access the full employee list.

  • Use the Search bar to find an employee by name, email, or employee number.
  • Filter by Status (active / inactive), Department, Gender, or Hire Date range.
  • Click any employee row to open their full profile.
  • Use the Export CSV button to download the filtered list for payroll or reporting purposes.
  • Bulk actions allow you to activate, deactivate, or reassign multiple employees at once.
Fig 3 — Employee Directory with search, filters, and bulk action controls.
➕

Create & Edit Employee

Onboarding a new employee involves filling in their personal details, employment information, and financial/banking data. The system auto-generates an employee number based on the branch code. Non-admin HR staff must submit an approval reason — the record enters a pending state until approved by a super admin.

1

Creating a New Employee Record

Navigate to Create Employee in the sidebar and complete the multi-section form.

  1. Fill in Personal Details: first name, last name, gender, date of birth, phone, address.
  2. Fill in Employment Details: department, job title, hire date, employment type (full-time / part-time / contract), shift.
  3. Fill in Financial Details: base salary, bank name, account number, account name.
  4. Upload a Profile Photo (optional).
  5. Click Save Employee. If you are not a super admin, an approval modal appears — enter a reason and submit for review.
⚠️
Approval Workflow: New employee records created by non-super-admin HR staff are held in a pending state. The record is visible but marked inactive until a super admin approves it via the Audit Management module.
Fig 4 — Create Employee form with personal, employment, and financial detail sections.
2

Editing an Existing Employee Record

Click the Edit icon on any employee row in the directory, or open their profile and click Edit.

  • All fields from the create form are editable. Change only the fields that need updating.
  • Every edit is tracked in the audit log — you can view change history from the employee profile.
  • Role assignments can also be managed from the edit form via the Roles section.
Fig 5 — Edit Employee form pre-filled with existing data. All changes are audit-logged.

Employee Profile

The Employee Profile is a read-only view of a staff member's complete record. It is organised into tabs — Personal, Employment, Finance, and Leave — giving HR a full picture without the risk of accidental edits. Use it for audits, leave queries, and onboarding verification.

1

Reading the Employee Profile Tabs

Click any employee's name in the directory to open their profile.

  • Personal tab: full name, gender, date of birth, phone, address, profile photo.
  • Employment tab: employee number, department, job title, hire date, employment type, status, assigned shifts.
  • Finance tab: base salary, hourly rate, bank account details for payroll processing.
  • Leave tab: current leave balances by type, leave history for the current year, and any pending leave applications.
💡
The Leave tab on the employee profile is the quickest way to check how many leave days a specific employee has remaining without navigating to the Leave Balance page.
Fig 6 — Employee Profile with Personal, Employment, Finance, and Leave tabs.

Leave Types

Leave Types define the rules for each category of leave available to employees — such as Annual Leave, Sick Leave, Maternity Leave, and Casual Leave. Each type has its own default allocation, payment status, maximum consecutive days, minimum notice period, and whether supporting documentation is required.

1

Managing Leave Types

Navigate to Leave Types in the sidebar to view, create, or edit leave categories.

  • Each leave type row shows: name, default days/year, paid status, max consecutive days, and notice period.
  • Click Add Leave Type to create a new category.
  • Key fields when creating a leave type:
    • Default Days: number of days allocated per year (used for bulk allocation).
    • Paid: whether the employee receives full pay during this leave.
    • Max Consecutive Days: maximum days that can be taken in a single application.
    • Min Notice Period: minimum days advance notice required.
    • Requires Document: whether a supporting document (e.g., medical certificate) must be uploaded.
Fig 7 — Leave Types page showing all defined categories with their rules.
📋

Leave Allocations

Leave Allocations assign the annual leave entitlement to each employee. Once leave types are configured, HR uses this page to bulk-allocate the default days to all employees for the current year, or to manually adjust an individual's allocation. Without an allocation, an employee cannot apply for that leave type.

1

Allocating Leave to Employees

Navigate to Leave Allocations in the sidebar to manage annual entitlements.

  1. Select the Leave Type and Year from the filters.
  2. Click Bulk Allocate to automatically assign the leave type's default days to all eligible employees for that year.
  3. To adjust a specific employee's allocation, find their row and click Edit to set a custom number of days.
  4. A Note field is available to document the reason for any non-standard allocation.
⚠️
Annual Setup: Leave allocations must be refreshed at the start of each new calendar year. Run Bulk Allocate for each leave type at the beginning of January to ensure employees can apply for leave.
Fig 8 — Leave Allocations page for assigning annual leave entitlements to employees.
📝

Applying for Leave

Any employee can apply for leave through the self-service portal. The system validates their remaining balance, checks the notice period, and calculates working days automatically (excluding weekends). Once submitted, the request enters the HR approval queue.

1

Submitting a Leave Application

Navigate to Apply Leave and complete the form to request time off.

  1. Select the Leave Type (e.g., Annual, Sick, Casual).
  2. Pick the Start Date and End Date.
  3. The system calculates Working Days automatically, excluding weekends.
  4. Enter a Reason for the leave.
  5. Upload a Supporting Document if required by the leave type (e.g., medical certificate for sick leave).
  6. Click Submit Application. Your request is sent to HR for approval.
ℹ️
Validation: The system will reject your application if: you have insufficient balance remaining, the dates violate the minimum notice period, or the requested days exceed the maximum consecutive limit set for that leave type.
Fig 9 — Leave application form with leave type, date picker, and document upload.

My Leaves

The My Leaves page shows your personal leave application history — all requests you have submitted, their current status, and the option to cancel pending or approved requests. It is the go-to page for tracking what you have applied for and whether it has been approved.

1

Viewing Your Leave History

Navigate to My Leaves to see all your leave applications and their status.

  • Each row shows: leave type, start/end date, days requested, date applied, and status badge.
  • Status options: Pending Approved Rejected Cancelled
  • Click Cancel on a pending or approved request to withdraw it (you must provide a cancellation reason).
  • Cancelled leave days are automatically returned to your balance.
Fig 10 — My Leaves showing personal leave application history with status tracking.
⚖️

Leave Balance

The Leave Balance page shows your remaining entitlement for each leave type in the selected year. It displays allocated days, used days, pending days (approved but not yet taken), and the net balance available for new applications.

1

Checking Your Remaining Leave Balance

Navigate to Leave Balance to see how many days you have left for each leave type.

  • Select the Year from the dropdown to view balances for past or current years.
  • Each leave type row shows: allocated, used, pending, and available days.
  • The leave history at the bottom lists all applications that have consumed or reserved balance for the selected year.
💡
HR managers can also view any employee's leave balance from the employee profile's Leave tab — useful when processing leave approvals.
Fig 11 — Leave Balance showing allocated, used, pending, and available days by leave type.

Approving Leave

The Approve Leave page is the HR manager's queue for reviewing all pending leave applications from employees across the branch. Each application shows the employee's details, leave type, dates, and remaining balance so you can make an informed decision before approving or rejecting.

1

Processing a Leave Application

Navigate to Approve Leaves to see all pending employee leave requests.

  1. Review the pending applications list — each row shows employee name, leave type, dates, days requested, and remaining balance.
  2. Click Approve to approve the application. Optionally add an approval note.
  3. Click Reject to decline it. A rejection reason is required and will be shown to the employee.
  4. Approved leaves deduct from the employee's balance immediately. Rejected leaves return the pending days to their balance.
⚠️
Check Balance First: Before approving, confirm the employee has sufficient balance remaining. The system will warn you if balance is insufficient, but the final decision rests with you.
Fig 12 — Approve Leaves queue showing pending applications with employee details and balance.

Clock-In Board — Today

The Today Clock-In Board gives a real-time view of which employees have clocked in for the current working day. It shows clock-in time, punctuality status (on time / late / early), and a summary count at the top. Use it to monitor attendance at the start of each shift.

1

Monitoring Today's Attendance

Navigate to Today under Clock-In Board in the sidebar.

  • The summary bar at the top shows: Total Clocked In, Late, and Early counts.
  • Each row shows: employee name, department, expected start time, actual clock-in time, and punctuality badge.
  • Filter by Department or search by employee name to narrow the view.
  • Employees who have not clocked in yet are not shown — this page only lists those who are present.
💡
Refresh the page periodically during the start-of-shift period to see updated clock-in data as employees arrive. The board shows live data from the moment each employee clocks in via their terminal.
Fig 13 — Clock-In Board (Today) showing real-time attendance with punctuality status.

Attendance History

The Attendance History board shows clock-in and clock-out records for all employees across a configurable date range (default: last 7 days). It includes hours worked, punctuality statistics, and a CSV export for payroll and compliance audits.

1

Reviewing Historical Attendance Records

Navigate to All Employees (Date Range) under Clock-In Board.

  • Set the Start Date and End Date to define the reporting period.
  • Each row shows: employee name, date, clock-in time, clock-out time, hours worked, and punctuality status.
  • Summary statistics below the table show: total hours logged, late percentage, and average hours per day.
  • Click Export CSV to download the full dataset for payroll processing or compliance records.
  • To view a single employee's history, navigate to their profile and use the attendance section, or drill down from this board.
Fig 14 — Attendance History board with date range filter, clock times, and export option.

Shift Management

The Shift Management module lets HR configure shift types (morning, afternoon, night) and assign employees to specific shifts. Shifts define the expected clock-in window used by the attendance board to calculate punctuality. They also gate POS and production operations in the operational modules.

1

Viewing the Shift Dashboard

Navigate to Shift Dashboard to see current shift assignments by employee and department.

  • The dashboard shows all active shift assignments across departments.
  • Each row shows: employee name, department, and their assigned shift.
  • Use the department filter to narrow the view to a specific team.
Fig 15 — Shift Management dashboard showing employee-to-shift assignments.
⚙️

Shift Configuration

Shift Configuration is where HR defines the shift types available at the branch — their names, start times, end times, and applicable days. These configurations are used system-wide: in attendance tracking, POS session management, and production batch recording.

1

Defining Shift Types

Navigate to Shift Configuration to manage shift definitions.

  • Click Add Shift to create a new shift type.
  • Enter: shift name (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Night), start time, end time, and applicable days of the week.
  • Shift times determine the punctuality window used in attendance reporting.
  • Changes to shift times take effect from the next working day.
⚠️
Operational Impact: Shift types are referenced by the POS, Production, and Sales modules. Changing shift names or times can affect active shifts in other departments — coordinate with department managers before making changes.
Fig 16 — Shift Configuration showing defined shift types with start/end times.

Shift Assignment

Shift Assignment links individual employees to a specific shift type. Once assigned, the employee's shift is reflected in attendance monitoring and their daily workflow gates in POS and production. Reassigning an employee changes their shift effective immediately.

1

Assigning Employees to Shifts

Navigate to Shift Assignment to link employees to defined shift types.

  1. Select the Employee from the dropdown (search by name).
  2. Select the Shift you want to assign them to.
  3. Optionally set an Effective Date for when the new shift starts.
  4. Click Assign. The employee's shift is updated across all modules.
Fig 17 — Shift Assignment page for linking employees to their designated shift.

Employee Appraisals

The Appraisals module enables structured performance reviews. HR creates appraisal cycles (defined periods), then conducts individual appraisals for each employee — rating their performance, documenting strengths and areas for improvement, and setting goals. Historical appraisals are retained for trend analysis.

1

Viewing the Appraisals Dashboard

Navigate to Employee Appraisals to see an overview of all performance reviews.

  • The dashboard lists all employees with their most recent appraisal rating and date.
  • Filter by department or search by employee name.
  • Click Appraise next to an employee to open the appraisal form.
  • Click History to view all past appraisals for that employee.
Fig 18 — Employee Appraisals dashboard with rating overview and action buttons.
2

Conducting an Appraisal

Click Appraise next to an employee to open the performance review form.

  1. Select the Appraisal Cycle this review belongs to.
  2. Rate the employee on each criterion using the 1–5 scale (1 = Poor, 5 = Outstanding).
  3. Add Comments for overall performance.
  4. Document Strengths — specific achievements and positive behaviours.
  5. Document Areas for Improvement — constructive feedback with actionable suggestions.
  6. Set Goals for the next review period.
  7. Click Submit Appraisal. The record is saved and included in performance analytics.
💡
Appraisals are linked to a specific cycle. Ensure you select the correct active cycle before submitting — you cannot change the cycle once an appraisal is saved.
Fig 19 — Appraisal form with rating scales, comments, strengths, improvement areas, and goals.
3

Viewing Appraisal History

Click History on any employee to see all their past appraisals.

  • History is listed chronologically with the most recent appraisal at the top.
  • Each entry shows the cycle name, date, overall rating, and the appraiser's name.
  • Expand any entry to read the full comments, strengths, areas for improvement, and goals set.
Fig 20 — Appraisal History showing an employee's full performance review timeline.
🔄

Appraisal Cycles

Appraisal Cycles define the review periods — for example, Q1 2025, Mid-Year 2025, or Annual Review 2025. Before you can conduct any appraisals, an active cycle must exist. Cycles have a status of Planned, Active, or Completed.

1

Managing Appraisal Cycles

Navigate to Appraisal Cycles to create, activate, or close review periods.

  1. Click New Cycle to create a review period.
  2. Enter: cycle name, start date, end date, and due date (deadline for completing appraisals).
  3. Optionally scope the cycle to a specific department.
  4. Set the initial status to Planned.
  5. When ready to begin reviews, change the status to Active.
  6. Once all appraisals are complete, mark the cycle as Completed.
ℹ️
Only Active cycles appear in the appraisal form dropdown. Always ensure a cycle is set to Active before asking managers to conduct their team reviews.
Fig 21 — Appraisal Cycles showing defined review periods with status tracking.

Payroll Hub

The Payroll Hub is the central dashboard for all payroll activity. It shows pending payroll runs, standalone payslips, and key stats — total runs, pending approvals, and total amount paid this month. From here you navigate to create new payroll runs or standalone payslips.

1

Reading the Payroll Hub

Navigate to All Payroll in the sidebar to access the payroll dashboard.

  • The Runs tab lists all payroll runs — bulk salary payments processed for a pay period.
  • The Standalone tab lists individual payslips not part of a bulk run.
  • Status badges: Draft Approved Paid Cancelled
  • Summary cards at the top show total runs, pending approval count, and total paid this month.
Fig 22 — Payroll Hub showing runs and standalone payslips with status and totals.

Payroll Runs

A Payroll Run processes salaries for multiple employees in a single batch. You define the pay period, select employees, adjust any one-off amounts (overtime, allowances, deductions), preview the totals, and submit. The run goes through Draft → Approved → Paid stages, with GL entries posted automatically at each transition.

1

Creating a Payroll Run

Navigate to New Payroll Run to process bulk employee salaries.

  1. Enter a Run Name (e.g., "May 2025 Payroll").
  2. Set the Pay Period Start and Pay Period End dates.
  3. Set the Payment Date — when salaries will be disbursed.
  4. Add optional Notes for the run.
  5. In the employee table, toggle employees on or off using the checkboxes. Use Toggle All to select all.
  6. For each employee, review and adjust:
    • Base Salary — pre-filled from employee record.
    • Overtime Hours and Overtime Rate — for extra hours worked.
    • Allowances — bonuses or expense reimbursements.
    • Tax Deductions and Other Deductions.
  7. Review the Preview Totals (gross, net, employee count) at the bottom.
  8. Click Create Run to save it as a Draft.
ℹ️
Draft → Approved → Paid: After creating a draft run, review individual payslips, then click Approve All to post the salary accrual GL entries. Finally click Mark All Paid when payments have been disbursed — this posts the payment GL entries.
Fig 23 — Create Payroll Run form with employee selection, pay period, and per-employee salary fields.
🧾

Standalone Payslips

A standalone payslip is created for a single employee outside of a bulk payroll run — useful for contract payments, bonuses, or corrective payroll adjustments. It follows the same Draft → Approved → Paid lifecycle as payroll runs with individual GL postings.

1

Creating a Standalone Payslip

Navigate to New Payslip in the sidebar to process a single employee payment.

  1. Select the Employee — their base salary and hourly rate load automatically from their record.
  2. Set the Pay Period start and end dates and the Payment Date.
  3. Select the Bank Account the payment will be drawn from.
  4. Review the salary components preview (base, overtime, allowances, deductions, gross, net).
  5. Adjust any components as needed for this specific payment.
  6. Click Create Payslip to save as Draft, then proceed through Approve → Mark Paid.
Fig 24 — Standalone Payslip form for processing a single employee payment with salary preview.

HR Reports

The HR Reports Hub provides eight pre-built analytical reports covering every aspect of the workforce. Select a report type, set a date range, and generate live data. Reports can be saved, searched, and submitted for management review.

1

Generating an HR Report

Navigate to HR Reports in the sidebar to access the reporting hub.

  1. Select the Report Type from the dropdown. Available types:
    • Workforce Overview — headcount by department, branch, and status.
    • Leave Utilization — leave taken vs allocated by type and department.
    • Attendance & Punctuality — clock-in patterns, late rates, hours worked.
    • Payroll & Compensation — salary totals, deductions, net pay by department.
    • Turnover & Retention — hiring, resignations, and retention rates over time.
    • Appraisal & Performance Ratings — rating distributions and trends.
    • Leave Balance & Accrual — remaining balance summary across all employees.
    • Department Staffing & Workload — headcount vs shift coverage by department.
  2. Select the Period (today, this week, this month, last month, or custom date range).
  3. Click Generate to load the report preview.
  4. Click Save Report to store it in the saved reports list below.
  5. Use Submit for Review to send the report to a senior manager.
Fig 25 — HR Reports Hub with report type selector, period filter, and saved reports list.
2

Workforce Overview Report

The Workforce Overview is one of the most commonly used HR reports — it shows headcount distribution across departments and branches.

  • Shows total active headcount, breakdown by department, and employment type distribution.
  • Charts visualise department sizes and gender distribution.
  • Use it for board presentations, budget planning, and staffing gap analysis.
Fig 26 — Workforce Overview Report showing headcount by department with distribution charts.

Report Generator

The Report Generator is a branch-level reporting tool that allows HR managers to compose, compile, and send formal reports to the Managing Director. Unlike the HR Reports hub (which generates live analytics), this module produces structured narrative reports for management review.

1

Generating and Submitting Reports

Navigate to Generate Reports in the sidebar under the Reporting section.

  • Select the report template and date range.
  • The generator pulls live data from the system to populate the report structure.
  • Review the generated content, add narrative comments where needed.
  • Use Compile Reports to bundle multiple reports together.
  • Use Send to Managing Director to dispatch the final compiled report to the MD for review.
ℹ️
The Send to Managing Director function requires the send-reports-to-md permission. HR Managers have this permission by default.
Fig 27 — Report Generator for creating, compiling, and sending formal management reports.

Roles & Permissions

The Roles & Permissions page lets HR managers view the full permission set for each system role. While role creation and permission assignment are restricted to super admins, HR managers can view current role definitions and submit change requests through the audit workflow.

1

Viewing Roles and Their Permissions

Navigate to Roles & Permissions to see the full permission matrix for each system role.

  • Roles are listed (Super Admin, Admin, Manager, Supervisor, Staff) with their assigned permissions.
  • Each permission is shown as a checkbox — active permissions are checked.
  • Filter by Guard (web or employee) to see the correct permission set.
  • To request a permission change, submit an audit request with the reason — a super admin must approve all role modifications.
⚠️
Core Roles Protected: Built-in system roles (Super Admin, Admin, etc.) cannot be deleted. Modifications to these roles require super admin approval and are audit-logged permanently.
Fig 28 — Roles & Permissions showing role definitions and their permission assignments.

Quick Reference

Use this section as your daily HR operations checklist and quick lookup for every module page.

1

Daily HR Workflow Checklist

Follow this sequence each working day for consistent, audit-ready HR management.

  1. Check Clock-In Board (Today) — monitor who has clocked in and flag absentees.
  2. Review Approve Leaves — process any pending leave applications before the shift starts.
  3. Check shift assignments if any rosters have changed.
  4. Process new employees via Create Employee if onboarding is due.
  5. Run payroll on pay-period close days — create run, approve, mark paid.
  6. Generate HR Reports for weekly or monthly management review.
  7. Update appraisals when a review cycle is active — complete before the due date.
