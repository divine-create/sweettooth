# Demo Scripts — Reporting & MD Reports

**Role:** The reporting pipeline runs across departments and ends with the Managing
Director. The flow is always the same: **Generate → Review → Compile → Send to MD →
MD reviews.** Department managers generate and submit; a reviewer approves; reports
are compiled into one document and sent up; the MD reads and responds.

> **Note:** Each department only generates reports for its own category (a Production
> Manager sees production reports, and so on). The "Send to Managing Director" step
> needs the send-reports permission.

**Total videos in this role:** 6

---

### Section 1 — How reporting works (overview)
**Estimated length:** ~2:00
**Screen / on-screen actions:**
1. Show the Reporting menu: Generate, Review, Compile, Send to MD.
2. Draw the flow on screen (or use a simple slide): Generate → Review → Compile →
   Send to MD → MD reviews.
3. Briefly show the MD Reports dashboard at the end of the chain.

**Narration:**
> Welcome to SweetTooth ERP training. In this video, we'll explain how reporting
> works from end to end, before we do each step in detail.
>
> Reporting in SweetTooth follows a clear pipeline with four stages. First, a
> department generates a report from live system data. Second, that report is
> reviewed and approved. Third, one or more approved reports are compiled into a
> single document. And fourth, the compiled document is sent up to the Managing
> Director, who reads it and can respond.
>
> Every report is tied to a department category — production, sales, inventory,
> accounting, or human resources — so each team works with its own reports. The
> important thing to remember is the order: generate, review, compile, send. We'll now
> walk through each stage.

### Section 2 — Generating a report
**Estimated length:** ~3:00
**Screen / on-screen actions:**
1. Go to **Reporting → Generate**.
2. Pick a report from the grouped list (by category).
3. Set the period (from / to date) and department if asked.
4. Click **Generate preview**; show the summary metrics, charts, and tables.
5. Click **Generate report** to save it as a draft.
6. Click **Submit for review**.

**Narration:**
> In this video, we'll generate a report.
>
> Open Reporting, then Generate. You'll see the reports available to you, grouped by
> category. Pick the one you want. Set the period you're reporting on — a start date
> and an end date — and choose the department if you're asked.
>
> Before saving anything, click Generate Preview. The system pulls live data and shows
> you what the report will contain: the key metrics at the top, charts, and detailed
> tables. This lets you check the figures look right.
>
> When you're happy, click Generate Report. This saves it as a draft. A draft on its
> own doesn't go anywhere, so the final step is to submit it for review. Once you
> submit, it moves to the review stage and is locked, so the numbers can't change
> underneath it.

---

### Section 3 — Reviewing and approving reports
**Estimated length:** ~2:30
**Screen / on-screen actions:**
1. Go to **Reporting → Review**.
2. Show reports with their statuses (pending review, reviewed, rejected).
3. Open a pending report; read the content.
4. Add an annotation / note.
5. Approve it (marks as reviewed); then show rejecting one with a reason.

**Narration:**
> In this video, we'll review and approve reports.
>
> Open Reporting, then Review. This screen lists reports and their status — which are
> waiting for review, which have been approved, and which were rejected. Open a report
> that's pending and read through it to check it's accurate and complete.
>
> If you have a comment, add an annotation — a note attached to the report that the
> author can see. If everything is in order, approve it. That marks it as reviewed and
> records you as the reviewer, which means it's now ready to be compiled.
>
> If something's wrong, reject it instead and give a reason. The report goes back so it
> can be corrected and regenerated. This review step is what keeps poor or mistaken
> numbers from ever reaching the Managing Director.

---

### Section 4 — Compiling reports
**Estimated length:** ~2:30
**Screen / on-screen actions:**
1. Go to **Reporting → Compile**.
2. Select multiple reviewed reports (same branch).
3. Give the compilation a title, description, and period.
4. Add an executive summary and recommendations.
5. Click **Compile**; show the resulting compiled report.

**Narration:**
> In this video, we'll compile reviewed reports into a single document.
>
> Open Reporting, then Compile. Here you select the approved reports you want to bundle
> together — for example, the week's production, sales, and inventory reports. They all
> need to be from the same branch and already reviewed.
>
> Give the compilation a clear title, a description, and the period it covers. You can
> also write an executive summary and add your recommendations, so the Managing
> Director gets the headline message along with the detail.
>
> Click Compile, and the system creates one combined report from everything you
> selected, and marks those individual reports as compiled. This compiled document is
> what goes up the chain.

---

### Section 5 — Sending to the Managing Director
**Estimated length:** ~1:30
**Screen / on-screen actions:**
1. Go to **Reporting → Send to MD**.
2. Select the compiled report.
3. Confirm the MD recipient.
4. Click send; show the status change to "sent to MD".

**Narration:**
> In this short video, we'll send a compiled report to the Managing Director.
>
> Open Reporting, then Send to Managing Director. Select the compiled report you
> prepared. The system finds the Managing Director automatically — if there's just
> one, it's selected for you. Confirm and send.
>
> The report's status changes to "sent to Managing Director", and it now appears on
> the Managing Director's dashboard. Note that this step needs the send-reports
> permission, so usually a manager or department head does it.

---

### Section 6 — The MD reviewing reports
**Estimated length:** ~2:30
**Screen / on-screen actions:**
1. Log in as MD (or switch to MD view); go to **MD Reports → Dashboard**.
2. Show compiled reports with status "sent to MD".
3. Open one; read the executive summary, metrics, and recommendations.
4. Add MD feedback.
5. Mark as reviewed; show status change to "reviewed by MD".

**Narration:**
> In this final reporting video, we'll see the Managing Director's side.
>
> Logged in as the Managing Director, open the MD Reports dashboard. Every compiled
> report that's been sent up appears here. Open one, and you get the whole picture in
> one place — the executive summary, the key metrics, the supporting detail, and the
> team's recommendations.
>
> If the Managing Director wants to respond — to ask a question or give direction —
> they add their feedback directly on the report, which the department can see. When
> they're done, they mark it as reviewed, and the status updates to "reviewed by
> Managing Director". That closes the loop: the report has gone from raw data all the
> way to an executive decision, entirely inside the system. That completes the
> Reporting videos, and the full set of role training videos.
