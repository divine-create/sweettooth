# Demo Scripts — HR Manager

**Role:** Level 3. Full control over people management: employees, departments,
shifts, leave, payroll, and role assignment. No access to Accounting, Inventory, or
Production operations.

**Total videos in this role:** 8

---

### Section 1 — HR dashboard tour
**Estimated length:** ~1:30
**Screen / on-screen actions:**
1. Log in as HR; land on the HR dashboard.
2. Pan across the HR menu: Employees, Departments, Shifts, Leave, Payroll,
   Appraisals, Clock-In Board, Roles.
3. Point out any summary widgets (headcount, on leave today, clocked in).

**Narration:**
> Welcome to SweetTooth ERP training. In this video, we'll tour the Human Resources
> module.
>
> When you log in as HR, your dashboard gives you a quick read on the workforce — how
> many employees you have, who's on leave today, and who has clocked in.
>
> From the menu you can reach everything you manage: employee records, departments,
> shift schedules, leave, payroll, performance appraisals, the clock-in board, and
> role assignments. We'll walk through each of these in the videos that follow.

---

### Section 2 — Managing employees
**Estimated length:** ~3:00
**Screen / on-screen actions:**
1. Go to **Employee Management**. Show the employee list with search and filters.
2. Click **Add employee**. Fill in name, employee number, department, employment
   status, contact details. Save.
3. Open an employee profile; show the tabs (details, employment, appraisals,
   probation).
4. Edit a field and save. Show how to mark an employee Inactive.

**Narration:**
> In this video, we'll manage employee records.
>
> Open Employee Management. This is the master list of everyone who works at the
> branch. You can search by name and filter by department or status.
>
> To add someone, click "Add employee". Enter their name and a unique employee
> number, assign them to a department, set their employment status, and add their
> contact details. Then save.
>
> Click any employee to open their profile. Here you'll find their personal and
> employment details, along with their appraisal history and probation reviews. To
> make a change, edit the field and save.
>
> When someone leaves the business, don't delete them — set them to Inactive instead.
> That removes them from active lists and stops them logging in, while keeping their
> full history in the system.

### Section 3 — Departments
**Estimated length:** ~2:00
**Screen / on-screen actions:**
1. Go to **Departments**. Show the list grouped by category (Production, Sales, Support).
2. Create a department; assign it a category.
3. Edit an existing department.
4. Briefly show Department Categories.

**Narration:**
> In this video, we'll set up departments.
>
> Open Departments. Every department belongs to a category — Production, Sales, or
> Support. The category matters because the system uses it to decide which workflows
> apply. For example, Production and Sales departments are tied to the shift and
> dispatch processes.
>
> To create a department, click add, give it a name, and choose its category. To
> change one, open it and edit. You can also manage the categories themselves if you
> need a new grouping. Keeping departments accurate is important, because every
> employee, product, and sale is scoped to a department.

---

### Section 4 — Shift management
**Estimated length:** ~2:30
**Screen / on-screen actions:**
1. Go to **Shift Management → Configuration**. Show the configured shifts (Morning,
   Afternoon, Evening, Full-time) with start times.
2. Edit a shift's time. Save.
3. Go to **Shift Management → Assignment**. Assign an employee to a default shift type.

**Narration:**
> In this video, we'll configure shifts and assign staff to them. This is one of the
> most important HR setup tasks, because almost every operational screen in the
> system requires an active shift.
>
> Open Shift Management, then Configuration. The branch comes with a set of shifts —
> for example Morning, Afternoon, Evening, and Full-time — each with a start time.
> Adjust these times to match the branch's real working hours, and save.
>
> Now go to Assignment. Here you set each employee's default shift type. Once an
> employee is assigned, they'll be able to clock in and select their shift, which
> unlocks the work screens for inventory, production, or sales. Without a shift, staff
> can't begin their day, so make sure every active employee is assigned.

---

### Section 5 — Leave management
**Estimated length:** ~3:00
**Screen / on-screen actions:**
1. Go to **Leave Management → Leave Types**. Show types (Annual, Sick, etc.).
2. Go to **Manage Allocations**. Allocate annual leave to an employee for the year.
3. Go to **Leave Requests / Approvals**. Open a pending request, approve it.
4. Show an employee's leave balance.

**Narration:**
> In this video, we'll handle leave.
>
> Start with Leave Types. These define the kinds of leave staff can take — for
> example Annual Leave and Sick Leave. Confirm the types match the business's policy.
>
> Next, go to Manage Allocations. This is where you give each employee their
> entitlement for the year — say, twenty-one days of annual leave. The system tracks
> the balance as leave is taken.
>
> When a staff member applies for leave, the request comes to you for approval. Open
> the pending request, review the dates, and approve or decline it. Once approved, the
> days are deducted from that employee's balance, which you can check at any time on
> their record.

### Section 6 — Clock-in board and attendance
**Estimated length:** ~1:30
**Screen / on-screen actions:**
1. Go to **Clock-In Board → Today**. Show who has clocked in.
2. Switch to **All-time** and **per-employee history**.
3. Point out auto clock-out behaviour if visible.

**Narration:**
> In this video, we'll review attendance using the clock-in board.
>
> Open the Clock-In Board. The Today view shows everyone who has clocked in for the
> current day, and when. This is your live picture of who's at work.
>
> You can switch to the all-time view, or drill into a single employee's history to
> see their attendance pattern over time. If a staff member forgets to clock out, the
> system can clock them out automatically at the end of their shift, so the records
> stay clean.

---

### Section 7 — Running payroll
**Estimated length:** ~3:00
**Screen / on-screen actions:**
1. Go to **Payroll**. Show past payroll runs.
2. Start a new payroll run; select the period and employees.
3. Show calculated payslips with earnings and deductions.
4. Finalise the run; open an individual payslip.
5. Mention the GL posting that happens automatically.

**Narration:**
> In this video, we'll run payroll.
>
> Open the Payroll screen. You'll see your history of past payroll runs. To start a
> new one, create a run and select the pay period and the employees to include.
>
> The system calculates each person's pay — their earnings and any deductions — and
> produces a payslip. Review the figures before you finalise. When you finalise the
> run, the payslips are issued, and the payroll automatically posts to the general
> ledger in the accounting module, so the finance team doesn't have to enter it by
> hand.
>
> You can open any individual payslip to view or print it for the employee.

---

### Section 8 — Appraisals and sending reports to the MD
**Estimated length:** ~2:30
**Screen / on-screen actions:**
1. Go to **Appraisals**. Show appraisal cycles and performance goals.
2. Open an employee's appraisal; show how it's recorded.
3. Go to HR **Reports** (workforce overview, leave utilisation).
4. Compile a report and use **Send to MD**.

**Narration:**
> In this final HR video, we'll cover appraisals and reporting.
>
> Open Appraisals. Here you manage appraisal cycles and set performance goals for
> staff. During a cycle, you record each employee's appraisal against their goals,
> building a performance history on their record.
>
> HR also produces reports — for example a workforce overview, and leave utilisation.
> When a report is ready, you compile it and use "Send to Managing Director". It then
> appears on the MD's reports dashboard for review. That completes the Human Resources
> videos.
